# About

Preferred Frame is an independent research lab with teams across the world.

We study foundational questions in physics, computation, AI, and formal systems.

We favor simple, transparent models over inherited abstractions, and we treat
experiment as the final test of truth.

We are human-*first* journal, in our publications are accepted as plain-text
files in the markdown md specification: [The PNP Markdown Standard – PNPMD](https://preferredframe.com/prints/The%20PNP%20Markdown%20Standard%20-%20PNPMDv1/10.5281/zenodo.17555930/The%20PNP%20Markdown%20Standard%20-%20PNPMDv1.html)

Team profiles: [preprints.preferredframe.com/_team](https://preprints.preferredframe.com/_team)
